Campus Wi-Fi Tips-Dead Zones

Click on any question below to see tips and senior troubleshooting advice.

How do I connect to campus Wi-Fi for the first time?

Select the Wi-Fi network name: Campus_Student. When the login portal page pops up in your browser, enter your college roll number as username and your registration birthdate as the initial password.

Where are the fastest Wi-Fi download speeds on campus?

The highest speeds (around 40 Mbps to 60 Mbps) are inside the Computer Science labs and on the Central Library ground floor directly near the access routers.

Why does mobile network and Wi-Fi disconnect in basement labs?

The Mechanical and Civil workshops are located in lower basement levels with thick reinforced concrete and metal beams that block radio waves. Make sure to download your PDF manuals and assignment sheets before entering!

How many devices can a student register at one time?

Each student account is limited to two active devices (one laptop and one smartphone). If you switch to a new phone, visit the IT support desk on 1st floor Admin Block to reset your MAC address.

What should I do if the login portal page does not open automatically?

Open your web browser (Chrome or Edge) and manually type 1.1.1.1 or http://wifi.college.local in the address bar to force the login portal to show up.
