About the College

krinu College of Engineering and Arts was founded in the year 1995 and is located in the central educational hub of Bangalore, Karnataka. The college is recognized for its academic excellence, disciplined environment, and practical industry-oriented teaching methodology.

The campus is spread across 25 acres of lush green surroundings, providing an ideal, peaceful, and noise-free learning atmosphere. With spacious lecture halls, modern auditoriums, and open seating zones, students enjoy a vibrant campus life balanced with academic rigor.

Courses Offered

The institution offers various undergraduate and postgraduate degree programs across Engineering, Management, and Science streams:

  • B.E. Computer Science and Engineering - 4 years
  • B.E. Electronics and Communication - 4 years
  • B.E. Mechanical Engineering - 4 years
  • B.E. Information Technology - 4 years
  • BCA (Bachelor of Computer Applications) - 3 years
  • B.Sc. Computer Science - 3 years
  • BBA (Bachelor of Business Administration) - 3 years
  • MBA (Master of Business Administration) - 2 years

Why Choose This College?

  • Experienced and supportive faculty members with doctoral and research backgrounds.
  • Fully equipped computing, electronics, and engineering workshops for hands-on practical training.
  • Active campus placement cell offering training sessions and tie-ups with leading tech companies.
  • A central library stocked with over 40,000 reference books, journals, and digital research portals.
  • Vibrant student community featuring cultural fests, technical clubs, coding forums, and sports tournaments.
  • Affordable tuition fee structure with merit-based scholarships available for eligible students.

Campus Facilities

  • Central Library with pin-drop silent reading rooms and online book search systems.
  • Multi-cuisine student canteen serving clean, hygienic, and affordable meals throughout the day.
  • On-campus boys and girls hostels with 24/7 security, study tables, and power backup.
  • Air-conditioned computer laboratories connected with high-speed optical fiber LAN.
  • Outdoor sports ground for cricket, football, and running, alongside indoor badminton courts.
  • Free high-speed campus-wide student Wi-Fi coverage across all lecture buildings.
